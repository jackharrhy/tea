hall-floor-wood.webp, hall-green-wood.webp, hall-trim-wood.webp
Source: Poly Haven, Wood Table 001
https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_001
Original: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/wood_table_001/wood_table_001_diff_1k.jpg
Photography: Dimitrios Savva. Processing: Rico Cilliers.
License: CC0 1.0 Universal https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
1024px photographed wood diffuse; same source credited in SOURCES.txt.
Downloaded 2026-11-08. All derivatives are 1024px, locally served WebP.

Floor: retains the 4 × 4 metre block pattern: 1 × 0.25 metre boards with
staggered joints. Each board takes a seeded crop of the photographed grain,
rotates it along the board and applies a muted grey-brown stain. Seed 47;
254 × 62px board interiors, 2px joints, alternating 0/128/64/192px row offsets.
WebP quality 91, roughness 0.92, shallow 4mm luminance bump.

Green panels: photographed luminance tinted green, retaining pores and knots
beneath the paint; base RGB 39/63/49, grain strength 0.65, WebP quality 90.
Brown trim: same grain rotated along horizontal rails; base RGB 93/67/43,
grain strength 0.85, WebP quality 90. Both use a 3mm luminance bump and
one-metre texture coverage on wall faces, rather than stretching across walls.
The grain tint multiplier is 1 + (luminance - 65) / 125 × strength.
Paint remains green; trim stays brown. No geometry or collision changes.
